## Verification Notes

### Confirmed Facts
- **Authentic Site**: The Nazca Lines are genuine pre-Columbian archaeological remains
- **Dating**: Created between approximately 500 BCE and 500 CE by the Nazca culture
- **Methods**: Made using simple technology (stake and rope) available to ancient peoples
- **Purpose**: Multiple functions including astronomical markers, ceremonial pathways, water cult rituals
- **Human Creation**: Overwhelmingly demonstrated through archaeology, no alien involvement

### Debunked Claims
- **Alien Runways**: Thoroughly debunked; impractical and unnecessary
- **Advanced Technology**: Simple techniques demonstrated through experimental archaeology
- **Impossible to Create**: Modern recreations prove feasibility
- **Must See from Air**: Misleading; purpose may not have been visual

### Ongoing Research
- Exact functions of all designs still being studied
- New discoveries continuing with modern technology
- Integration of multiple theories for comprehensive understanding
- Long-term preservation strategies

---

**Research Guidelines**: Approach Nazca Lines as authentic, well-documented archaeological site. While fascinating and impressive, they are thoroughly understood within archaeological context. Clearly distinguish between evidence-based theories (astronomical, ceremonial, water cult) and debunked pseudoarchaeological claims (aliens). Emphasize the sophistication of Nazca culture and the human achievement represented. Use as opportunity to discuss proper archaeological method, importance of evidence, and problems with pseudoarchaeology. Balance wonder and respect for the achievement with accurate scientific understanding.
